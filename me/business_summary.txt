GARLICLO | NADER GARLIC — BUSINESS SUMMARY
Garliclo (غارليكلو) — مقشّر عالهوا مش بالميّ (Air-peeled, not water-peeled). No chopping. No peeling. No mess.

Garliclo, also known as Nader Garlic, is a Lebanese garlic supplier founded in 1980 by Joseph Nader. It does one thing and does it well: peeled garlic for businesses. Garlic is in almost every Lebanese dish, from toum and shawarma to marinades and grills, yet peeling it by hand is one of the slowest and messiest jobs in any kitchen. Garliclo takes that job off the table. Our promise: "You don't peel, we peel for you" (إنت ما تقشّر، نحنا منقشّر عنك). Our main factory is in Sin El Fil (Metn), and we deliver all over Lebanon.

Our signature, written right on our logo, is "air-peeled, not water-peeled" (مقشّر عالهوا مش بالميّ). Instead of knives, soaking, or chemicals, our machines use bursts of compressed air to lift the skin off each clove. The cloves stay whole, firm, and unbruised, there is no added water, and the garlic keeps its natural sharp flavor and lasts longer. We also offer trimmed cloves (مقمّع), meaning the hard root end has been cut off, so the garlic is 100% usable with zero prep. Chefs trust Garliclo because it keeps their recipes consistent plate after plate, with garlic quality all year long. Garliclo is always ready, which makes it a must-have when opening any food and beverage business.

WHO WE SERVE: businesses only. We supply restaurants (including shawarma and grill shops), industrial and central kitchens, butcheries, supermarkets, caterers and hotels, and food manufacturers (sauces, pickles, ready meals). We do not sell small quantities to households or individual home cooks.

Our mission is to save Lebanese kitchens hours of tedious work every week while keeping quality, hygiene, and taste consistent, at honest and transparent prices. We give customers a real choice: garlic we air-peel ourselves in our Sin El Fil factory, or imported peeled garlic from China at a lower price point, including a frozen option for long storage.

PACKAGING & STORAGE
We pack to order, in whatever quantity the customer needs. Fresh garlic is vacuum-sealed in 1 kg, 5 kg, or 10 kg bags, or in custom quantities for bulk orders. Frozen garlic comes in 1 kg bags packed in 10 kg cartons. Keep fresh air-peeled garlic refrigerated at 0-4 °C: it stays fresh for up to 3 weeks unopened, and should be used within 3-5 days once opened. Keep frozen garlic at -18 °C for up to 12 months; thaw it in the fridge and do not refreeze. Fresh garlic is best for raw uses such as toum; frozen garlic is ideal for cooking, sauces, and marinades.
